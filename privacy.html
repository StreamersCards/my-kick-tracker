Privacy Policy

This site is intended to display public profile information about Kick channels, public social links, and public chat activity that are already visible on public-facing pages.

What we collect

We do not collect passwords, private messages, payment data, or private account information. We may store public profile data needed to display channel records, follower counts, social links, historical changes, and public chat messages. Public chat records may include a sender's public username, public user ID, message text, channel, message type, and timestamp.

How data is used

Public data may be used to show channel summaries, follower histories, profile metadata, public chat history, and historical records on this site. The site does not require login or account access.

Retention

Public profile and chat records may be retained as historical logs so changes and activity can be reviewed over time. Historical records are not automatically overwritten when newer data is collected.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not require cookies for core functionality and does not use invasive tracking or advertising scripts for user profiling.

Third-party content

The site may link to public Kick pages and public social profiles. Those third-party platforms may have their own privacy practices and terms.

Contact

If you need to request data removal or ask a privacy question, contact the site owner directly through the project or hosting contact channel.
